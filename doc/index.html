Build with FlexiTac

An open-source platform for tactile robotics, built around thin, flexible and scalable tactile sensors.

From sensor design and fabrication to tactile data, simulation and robot learning.

QuickstartResearch overview

  1. 01DesignFlexiTac-GenOptional

    FlexiTac on xArm

    Design a sensor that fits your own robot.

    Open FlexiTac-Gen

  2. 02BuildSensor + reader

    Build a sensor

    Two flexible PCBs around a pressure-sensitive film, under 3 minutes and about $4 in materials. A small reading board streams it over USB.

    Start building

  3. 03IntegrateRobot and data

    Mount it and collect data

    Teleoperate a robot with LeFlexiTac, or collect UMI data by hand with Touch in the Wild.

    Data collection

SimulateNo hardware required

With the standard sensor, skip Design. Simulation needs no hardware, so you can also start there.

Touch that fits your robot

A thin, flexible tactile array that follows the surface of your gripper, rigid or soft.

  • Flexible PCBsElectrodes printed on two 0.2 mm FPCs
  • Pressure-sensitive filmVelostat, sealed between them
  • Custom geometryFitted to your gripper with FlexiTac-Gen
