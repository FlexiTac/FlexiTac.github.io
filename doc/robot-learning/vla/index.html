#Available today

#LeFlexiTac: Pi0.5 and SmolVLA

Besides ACT and Diffusion Policy, LeFlexiTac trains Pi0.5 and SmolVLA on the LeRobot datasets you record with it, with or without touch (--policy.use_tactile=true / false). Fine-tuning Pi0.5 needs a GPU with at least 80 GB of memory. See steps 09–10 of the LeFlexiTac docs↗ and its results↗.
