#Available today

#LeFlexiTac: ACT and Diffusion Policy

LeFlexiTac trains policies on the LeRobot datasets you record with it. Touch is an extra input, switched on with --policy.use_tactile=true. On tube insertion, peg-in-hole and pen retrieval from a bag, the tactile policies beat their vision-only versions in every reported comparison (results↗).

Train with and without touch (--policy.use_tactile=true / false) on a Linux machine with a CUDA GPU, then roll the checkpoint out on the arm. Train and run a policy at the same frame rate you recorded at. Steps 09–10 of the LeFlexiTac docs↗ have the training and evaluation commands, and the same page ends with practical tips on calibration, camera placement and frame rates.

#Touch in the Wild: Diffusion Policy

Touch in the Wild trains a Diffusion Policy conditioned on its pretrained visuo-tactile encoder, then runs it on a uFactory xArm 850 with the same fingers and sensors.

To run policies you need: a uFactory xArm 850 with the xArm Gripper, the printed finger holder and GoPro mount from the gripper guide↗, an HDMI capture card for the GoPro feed, and an NVIDIA GPU for training. README: Training↗ · Real-world deployment↗
