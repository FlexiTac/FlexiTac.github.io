#On these pages

  • The 3D sensor on the Introduction is rendered from the V2 Gerbers on the UMI soft finger from HandUMI↗ (Apache-2.0), and shown with model-viewer↗ (Apache-2.0).
  • The FPC drawings, the exploded view and the colours of the boards follow Learn FlexiTac.
  • The "What you'll build" renders on Quickstart are made the same way: the sensor from the V2 Gerbers on the HandUMI finger, the reading board from its Gerbers and parts list, the Arduino Nano laid out as in Learn FlexiTac, and the FFC extenders modeled on Adafruit's product photos.
  • The sample clips on Data collection come from the LeFlexiTac↗ and Touch in the Wild↗ project pages.
  • The point cloud viewer and the tactile map on Tactile representation show one plug–socket demonstration replayed in FlexiTac-IsaacSim-Simulation↗ (Isaac Sim 6.2 and Isaac Lab 3.0): the ALOHA robot with UMI-style fingers, the WarpSdf tactile sensors, and an RTX camera for the pictures and the depth behind the camera points.
  • The xArm render in the Introduction's Design card comes from the FlexiTac-Gen library: the gripper is UFACTORY's 3D model, and the sensor is the FlexiTac V2 design.
